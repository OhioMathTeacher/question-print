3 · Who spoke, and which lines are questions

Tap T on the lines that are the questioner's (you, the teacher; or the AI, in a typed session) and S on the student's. Fix any words the transcriber got wrong by clicking into the text. Lines ending in a question mark are marked as questions; toggle any the transcriber missed or over-marked. Only the teacher's questions get tagged.

4 · Tag each question: what would the student have to do?

Judge by what answering would take, not by the verb. Depth of Knowledge is how far the thinking has to go; Bloom's is what kind of thinking. The matrix cell tells you what that combination looks like. Five combinations do not exist (shaded); the app will say so.

Tag them yourself first. Then let the machine try, and see where you part ways.
